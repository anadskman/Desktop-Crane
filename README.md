# Desktop Crane

I made a crane intended to sit on a desk.
I designed it similar to a robot arm with the wrist being a planetary joint for its torque and the elbow just being a nema 17.
The arm extentends using a rack and pinion design, and the hook is lowered by a cheap stepper motor turning a pully.

Note Im designing as CAD and i do not intend on buying the motors and assembling it.
